# VPR — Claude / AI Writing Rules

## No em dashes. Ever.

Do not use em dashes (—) anywhere in this project. Not in blog post copy, not in headings, not in comments, not in UI strings. Zero exceptions.

**Replace with:**
- Comma for parenthetical asides: "this is important, not optional, and enforced"
- Colon to introduce a list or explanation: "Step 1: Match the lot number"
- Period to break a long sentence into two
- "but" / "and" / "because" to join clauses naturally

**Wrong:** "A COA — even a polished one — can be fake."
**Right:** "A COA, even a polished one, can be fake."

**Wrong:** "Step 1 — Verify the lot number"
**Right:** "Step 1: Verify the lot number"

---

## General writing tone

- Write like a knowledgeable human, not a corporate brochure
- Short sentences over long ones
- No filler phrases: "it is worth noting", "it is important to", "in order to"
- Active voice preferred
- British/neutral English is fine; pick one style and stay consistent
